The Officer Line

Leadership in a Masonic lodge is service. Each officer holds a station with its own duties and lessons, and many will one day lead the lodge as Master. Together they guide our meetings, mentor new members and keep our traditions alive for the families who come after us.

Installation of Officers

Each year our new officers are installed in a public ceremony — one of the few times the lodge room is opened to families, friends and guests. It is a proud, memorable evening, and you are warmly invited.
