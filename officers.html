The Officer Line

Each officer holds a station with its own duties and symbolism, and together they conduct the business and ceremonial work of the lodge. Officers are installed each year in a public ceremony that families and friends are warmly invited to attend.

Installation of Officers

Our officers are installed in an open ceremony held at the close of each Masonic year. It is one of the few occasions when the lodge room is opened to the public, and we would be glad to have you join us.
