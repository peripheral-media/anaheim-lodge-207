What is Freemasonry

A fraternity for thoughtful men.

Freemasonry is one of the world's oldest fraternal organizations. It brings together men who believe in a higher power, value strong character, and want to improve themselves while contributing to the good of others.

Freemasonry is not a religion and does not replace one. Men of all faiths are welcome.

Who We Are

More than a meeting place.

While traditions are honored, the lodge continues to evolve with the needs of modern life. Men come here to learn from one another, build lasting friendships, and contribute positively to their families and communities.

What Freemasonry truly is.

Freemasonry is a fraternal organization that helps men become better versions of themselves through reflection, education, and service. Members believe in a higher power, though Freemasonry does not define or promote any religion. Men of all faiths are welcome.

Freemasonry values integrity, responsibility, and respect. It provides structure, mentorship, and fellowship in a world that often feels disconnected.

Men join for different reasons. Some seek friendship. Others seek purpose. Many find both.

Brotherhood & Service

A tradition of brotherhood and community service.

For more than a century, Anaheim Lodge No. 207 has served as a place where men gather to build character, strengthen friendships, and contribute meaningfully to their community. While Freemasonry is rooted in tradition, our focus is active and present. The lodge is not just a meeting place — it is a hub for service, mentorship, and civic involvement.

Men from many backgrounds come together here with a shared belief in personal responsibility, respect for others, and the importance of giving back.

Connections and community.

Service is central to who we are. Anaheim Lodge No. 207 proudly partners with local schools, nonprofit organizations, and community programs to make a tangible difference beyond our walls.

Through our partnership with Raising A Reader, we have helped promote childhood literacy and early education throughout California since 2010. We support students and educators through our connection with Benjamin Franklin Elementary School, recognizing students for high academic achievement each year, with additional programs and community initiatives continuing to grow.

Freemasonry across California has also become one of the largest contributors to the Los Angeles Angels RBI program. Through this partnership, Masons help provide baseball mitts so children in underserved communities can take part in T-ball and baseball, often for the first time.

These efforts reflect our belief that service should be visible, practical, and lasting.

Are You Ready?

The qualities we seek.

Freemasonry welcomes men from all walks of life, faiths, and backgrounds. We encourage you to reflect on the following:

A Belief in a Supreme Being

Freemasonry is not a religion, but it does require a belief in a Supreme Being. We respect and embrace all faiths.

Good Moral Character

We seek men who are upright, honest, and committed to the pursuit of moral excellence.

Of Lawful Age

In California, a man must be at least 18, though many find joining a few years later beneficial.

Able to Support Yourself & Your Family

Membership requires time and resources. Family and employment come first.

Seeking Knowledge & Self-Improvement

The desire to learn, grow, and continuously improve one's character.

Motivated by Free Will

Your decision must be your own — not for personal gain, but for genuine personal growth.

The Journey

The steps to becoming a Mason.

The process is deliberate and thoughtful — designed for mutual understanding. Your mentor will guide you at a steady pace.

01

Inquiry & Exploration

Start by exploring our website, reading about Freemasonry, and forming your questions.

02

Connecting with Our Lodge

Attend a public event or dinner, or reach out to arrange a casual conversation with a member.

03

The Petition

When you are ready, submit an application (a "petition") for membership to the lodge.

04

Investigation & Interview

A small committee meets with you to discuss motivations and qualifications. The conversation is friendly and informal.

05

The Ballot

Members vote privately on your petition, a tradition that ensures every brother stands behind your candidacy.

06

Receiving the Degrees

Entered Apprentice, Fellowcraft, and Master Mason — three symbolic ceremonies that convey our core teachings.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I have to be invited?
No. You are welcome to contact us directly and visit. Freemasonry is not a secret society.
Is Freemasonry a religion?
No. Freemasonry is not a religion and does not replace your faith. Men of all faiths are welcome.
What is the time commitment?
Early on, plan for degrees and short study sessions. Your mentor will pace it with you, respecting family and work obligations.
Is it secret?
Our ceremonies are private. Our values and community work are public.
What should I wear?
Business casual is fine for first visits unless otherwise noted.
How long does the process take?
It varies. Your mentor will help you progress at a steady, manageable pace.

What Are Masonic Youth Orders?

Building confidence, leadership, and a habit of service.

Masonic Youth Orders help young people grow through mentorship, ritual, and community service. Anaheim Lodge proudly supports the Walt Disney DeMolay Chapter and Job's Daughters Bethel No. 5.

Walt Disney DeMolay Chapter

DeMolay develops leadership, public speaking, teamwork, and service in young men ages 12–21. Members plan projects, hold offices, and grow through ritual and mentorship. Parents are welcome to learn more and attend open events.
