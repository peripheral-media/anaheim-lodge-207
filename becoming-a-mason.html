Your Path to Purpose

What it takes, what to expect, and how to take the first step with Anaheim Lodge No. 207.

Maybe you have been searching for a deeper sense of purpose. Maybe you want to be a better example for your children, or you miss the kind of friendship where men hold each other to a higher standard. Many of our members started exactly where you are.

Becoming a Freemason is a meaningful step, and it is never rushed. This page walks you through who we are looking for, how the process works, and how to start a no-pressure conversation with us.

Is Freemasonry Right for You?

Freemasonry welcomes men of every background, culture and faith. If the following describes you, we would be glad to meet you.

A Belief in a Supreme Being

Freemasonry is not a religion, but it does require a belief in a Supreme Being. We respect and embrace all faiths, and we never ask you to define yours.

Good Moral Character

We seek men who are upright and honest, and who are already engaged in the pursuit of moral excellence.

Of Lawful Age

In California a man must be at least 18 years of age. Many Lodges find that a little more life experience — 21 and above — is beneficial.

Able to Support Your Family

Membership asks for some time and modest dues. Your faith, your family and your livelihood always come first — we will tell you so plainly, and we mean it.

Seeking Self-Improvement

A genuine desire to learn, to grow, and to keep improving throughout your life is the quality that matters most.

Motivated by Free Will

Your decision must be your own, arrived at freely, and never made in expectation of personal or commercial gain.

Sound like you? The next steps are simple, and there is no pressure at any point.

Your Journey, Step by Step

The process is thoughtful by design. It gives you time to know us, and us time to know you.

Inquiry & Exploration

Start by exploring this website and reading about Freemasonry. Form your questions — all of them. There is nothing you cannot ask.

Connecting with Our Lodge

Come to a Wednesday dinner or a public event, or ask for a casual conversation with a member. Bring your spouse or a friend if you like. No obligation of any kind.

The Petition

When you are ready, you submit an application — traditionally called a petition — for membership.

Investigation & Interview

A small committee meets with you to discuss your motivations and qualifications. It is friendly and informal, usually over coffee.

The Ballot

Members vote privately on your petition, as they have for centuries.

Receiving the Degrees

Entered Apprentice, Fellowcraft, and Master Mason — three symbolic ceremonies that convey the core teachings of our fraternity.

Timelines vary. Your mentor will guide you at a steady, manageable pace.

Curious? Come to Dinner.

The best way to understand Freemasonry — and the warm, family spirit of Anaheim Lodge — is to sit down with us. Share a meal, meet our members and their families, and ask anything you like. There is no obligation.
