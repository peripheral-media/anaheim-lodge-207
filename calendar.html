When We Meet

Guests and families are welcome at our dinners and public events. Our lodge meetings are for members, but there is almost always something happening that you and your family can join.

Stated Meeting

  • First Monday of each month
  • Business of the Lodge
  • Members and visiting Masons

Wednesday Nights

  • Dinner at 6:30 PM, guests welcome
  • Degree work as candidates progress
  • Entered Apprentice, Fellowcraft, Master Mason

Public Events

  • Installation of Officers
  • Family dinners and holiday celebrations
  • School appreciation and charity activities

We gather on Wednesday nights with dinner at 6:30 PM on the second floor. Our Stated Meeting is held on the first Monday of each month.
