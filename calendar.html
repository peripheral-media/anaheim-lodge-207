Calendar

Stated meetings, degree nights, dinners, and community events — everything happening at 303 W Lincoln Avenue.

When We Meet

Anyone is welcome to come to a dinner or a public event. Tyled meetings are for members, but there is almost always something happening that you can join.

Stated Meeting

  • First Monday of each month
  • Business of the Lodge
  • Members and visiting brethren

Wednesday Nights

  • Dinner at 6:30 PM, guests welcome
  • Degree work as candidates progress
  • Entered Apprentice, Fellowcraft, Master Mason

Public Events

  • Installation of Officers
  • Family and community dinners
  • School and charity activities

We gather on Wednesday nights with dinner at 6:30 PM on the second floor. Our Stated Meeting is held on the first Monday of each month.
