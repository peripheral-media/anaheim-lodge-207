Youth Orders at Anaheim

Leadership, service, and character for young people — supported by Anaheim Lodge No. 207.

What Are Masonic Youth Orders?

Masonic youth orders help young people build confidence, leadership, and a lasting habit of service.

Anaheim Lodge proudly hosts and supports the Walt Disney DeMolay Chapter and Job's Daughters Bethel No. 5.

Explore each programme below to learn how to visit, join, and get involved. Both groups operate independently while sharing our values of moral development, leadership, and community service.

Walt Disney DeMolay Chapter

DeMolay develops leadership, public speaking, teamwork, and service in young men aged 12 to 21. Members plan projects, hold offices, and grow through ritual and mentorship. Parents are welcome to learn more and attend open events.

  • Age range: typically 12–21
  • Meets: day and time to be confirmed
  • Location: Anaheim Lodge No. 207
  • Contact: DeMolay Advisor
Contact the DeMolay Advisor

What They Do

  • Leadership offices and project planning
  • Community service and philanthropy
  • Public speaking and ritual team
  • Chapter events and regional meetings
  • Team building and social outings

How to Join

  • Inquire — send a note or attend an open event
  • Visit — meet members and see a meeting
  • Apply — complete the application with parent or guardian consent

For Parents

Youth protection guidelines are followed at all times, adult advisers are screened, and families are welcome at appropriate events under an open-door policy. We are glad to answer any question you have before your child attends.

Job's Daughters Bethel No. 5

Job's Daughters builds confidence, leadership, and community among girls and young women aged 10 to 20. Members lead meetings, plan service projects, and form lasting friendships in a values-driven setting. Parents are welcome to learn more and attend open events.

  • Age range: typically 10–20
  • Meets: day and time to be confirmed
  • Location: Anaheim Lodge No. 207
  • Contact: Bethel Guardian
Contact the Bethel Guardian

What They Do

  • Leadership roles and meeting offices
  • Community service and scholarship opportunities
  • Ritual and public presentation skills
  • Bethel events and regional activities
  • Team building and social gatherings

How to Join

  • Inquire — send a note or attend an open event
  • Visit — meet the Bethel and observe a meeting
  • Apply — complete the application with parent or guardian consent

For Parents

Youth protection guidelines are followed at all times, adult volunteers are screened, and families are welcome at appropriate events under an open-door policy. Questions are always welcome before your daughter attends.
