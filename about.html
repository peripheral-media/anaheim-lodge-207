Our Enduring Legacy in Orange County

Nestled in the heart of Anaheim, Anaheim Lodge No. 207 holds a unique and esteemed place in the history of Orange County. Chartered on 14 October 1870, we proudly stand as the oldest continuously operating Masonic Lodge in the entire county. For more than a century and a half our Lodge has been a beacon of light, character development, and community service.

We are a diverse brotherhood of men from all walks of life, united by shared values and a commitment to personal and collective betterment. Our rich history is matched only by our dedication to the future — fostering a supportive environment where members grow in wisdom, integrity, and fellowship.

From our historic meeting hall to our active presence in local initiatives such as the Benjamin Franklin Elementary School partnership, Anaheim Lodge No. 207 embodies the timeless principles of Freemasonry within a thriving, modern community. We invite you to be part of our continuing story.

What is Freemasonry?

Freemasonry is the world's oldest and largest fraternal organisation — a brotherhood dedicated to building better men through a system of moral and ethical lessons. Often misunderstood, it is neither a religion nor a secret society. It is a place where men of integrity, regardless of background or belief, come together to cultivate virtue, pursue knowledge, and contribute positively to their communities.

Our teachings are presented through allegory and symbol, encouraging thought and personal interpretation rather than dogmatic instruction. We believe that by making good men better, we collectively contribute to a better world.

At Its Core, Freemasonry Encourages

Four commitments shape everything that happens inside a lodge room, and a great deal of what happens outside it.

Live by a Moral Code

Emphasising honesty, compassion, integrity, and respect for all people, in every circumstance.

Engage in Self-Improvement

Through symbolic teaching and fellowship, members are inspired to continually refine their character and intellect.

Foster Brotherhood

Forming deep, lasting bonds built on mutual respect, trust, and shared ideals.

Practise Philanthropy

Actively participating in charitable work that benefits Anaheim and the wider community.
