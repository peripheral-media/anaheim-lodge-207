Why Men Join

Character, Family and Community

Freemasonry is the world's oldest fraternity, and its purpose is simple: to help good men become better ones. At Anaheim Lodge we do that together — around the dinner table, in the lodge room, and out in our community.

A Code Worth Living By

Our lessons are built on the four cardinal virtues — Temperance, Fortitude, Prudence and Justice — and on brotherly love, relief and truth. They are timeless, practical, and meant to be lived at home, at work and in the community.

A Family Lodge

Our wives, children and friends are part of lodge life. Weekly dinners, holiday gatherings and youth programs mean your family grows with you — among men of every background, faith and profession who look out for one another.

A Life of Meaning

Many men come to us at a turning point, looking for purpose beyond the daily routine. Here you will find mentors, honest conversation and room to reflect on the kind of man you want to be.

What Freemasonry Truly Is (and Isn't)

Search online and you will find plenty of mystery. The truth is far simpler. Freemasonry is a fraternity of men who believe in a Supreme Being and want to live with integrity. It is not a religion and never replaces your faith — members of many faiths sit side by side in our lodge. Our meetings are not secret; a few traditional words and signs are simply kept private, the way any old family keeps its traditions.

What we do in plain sight matters more: we support local schools, care for our members and their families, and give generously to Masonic charities that serve all of California. Questions are always welcome — ask us anything.

Serving Anaheim Since 1870

Relief — helping others — is one of the founding tenets of Freemasonry. For our lodge, that starts right here at home, with the schools, families and neighbors of Anaheim.

Nurturing Young Minds at Benjamin Franklin Elementary

Our lodge has a long-standing partnership with Benjamin Franklin Elementary School. Members volunteer their time and resources — school supplies, warm clothing and support for classroom programs — so local children have what they need to thrive. It is our favorite kind of work: hands-on, close to home, and lasting.

Events That Bring Us Together

From Wednesday night dinners to holiday parties, public school recognition nights and family celebrations, there is always a seat at our table. Many of our gatherings are open to guests — the easiest way to get to know us is to come and share a meal.

Raising the Next Generation of Leaders

A family lodge looks after its young people. Anaheim Lodge is proud to host and support two Masonic youth organizations where boys and girls build confidence, leadership and lifelong friendships — guided by adults who care.

Virtues every DeMolay strives to live by

Walt Disney Chapter, Order of DeMolay

Sponsored by Anaheim Lodge, the Walt Disney Chapter of DeMolay is part of an international organization that helps young men ages 12 to 21 become leaders of character. Members run their own activities, serve their community and build friendships — guided by caring adult advisors.

Learn More About DeMolay

Bethel #5, Job's Daughters International (Anaheim)

Bethel #5 of Job's Daughters International, which meets 1st & 3rd Tuesdays, gives young women ages 10 to 20 a supportive place to build confidence, speak in public, serve their community and form friendships that last a lifetime — all guided by a strong moral compass.
