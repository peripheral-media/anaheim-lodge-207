About

Our Foundation: Wisdom, Integrity, Community

At Anaheim Lodge No. 207, we stand on principles that foster personal transformation and collective good. We invite men from all walks of life to embark on a journey of self-discovery and shared purpose.

Beyond the Everyday

Freemasonry is a commitment to lifelong learning and moral development. Through allegorical teachings and reflective practice, members cultivate virtues that strengthen character and enrich every aspect of life.

A Family of Purpose

Find your place in a supportive and diverse brotherhood united by common values. We build lasting bonds through shared experiences, mutual respect, and a genuine desire to uplift one another.

Seeking Deeper Understanding

We encourage the pursuit of truth through open inquiry and reasoned discourse. Our discussions are rooted in timeless wisdom, guiding members to a clearer understanding of themselves and their role in the world.

What Freemasonry Truly Is (and Isn't)

The world wide web often paints a picture of mystery and secrecy when it comes to Freemasonry. At Anaheim Lodge, we believe in clarity and invitation. We are not a religion, nor do we seek to replace your faith. We are a fraternal organization founded on principles of brotherly love, relief, and truth. Our "secrets" are simply traditional modes of recognition and instruction — symbolic lessons designed to encourage moral and intellectual growth. We welcome honest inquiry and encourage you to explore the real purpose and positive impact of our ancient fraternity.

Making a Tangible Difference: Our Commitment to Anaheim

At Anaheim Lodge No. 207, our commitment to community upliftment is woven into the fabric of our brotherhood. We believe in active compassion and investing in the future of our city, right here in Orange County.

Nurturing Young Minds at Benjamin Franklin Elementary

We are proud to have a long-standing and impactful partnership with Benjamin Franklin Elementary School. From providing essential supplies and warm clothing to supporting educational initiatives, our members regularly volunteer their time and resources to enrich the lives of students and faculty. This direct involvement reflects our core value of relief, ensuring local children have the resources they need to thrive.

Events That Bring Us Together

Beyond specific projects, Anaheim Lodge hosts numerous family-friendly and community-focused events throughout the year. These gatherings foster fellowship, strengthen local bonds, and provide opportunities for everyone to experience the spirit of our fraternity in a welcoming environment. Check our calendar for upcoming opportunities to connect!

Mentoring Tomorrow's Leaders: Our Youth Connections

Freemasonry is dedicated to building character not just in men, but in the next generation. Anaheim Lodge No. 207 is proud to host and support vibrant youth organizations that empower young men and women with leadership skills, moral fortitude, and a sense of community.

Walt Disney Chapter, Order of DeMolay (Anaheim)

The Walt Disney Chapter of the Order of DeMolay, located right here at our Anaheim Lodge building, offers young men aged 12 to 21 invaluable opportunities for personal growth, leadership development, civic awareness, and social fun. DeMolay focuses on principles of character, respect, and responsibility.

Learn More About DeMolay

Bethel #5, Job's Daughters International (Anaheim)

For young women aged 10 to 20, Bethel #5 of Job's Daughters International, also based in Anaheim, provides a supportive environment for developing confidence, public speaking skills, patriotism, and a strong moral compass. They engage in charitable work and foster lifelong friendships.

Experience Orange County's Oldest Masonic Tradition.

We invite you to be part of this enduring tradition."
