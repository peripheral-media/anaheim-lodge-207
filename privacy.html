Privacy Policy

How Anaheim Lodge No. 207 handles the information you give us.

Draft for review. This page describes what this website actually does and collects. It has not been reviewed by an attorney. Please read it and confirm it matches your practice before the site goes live.

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information this website collects, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It applies only to this website. It does not cover the internal records of the Lodge, which are kept under the rules of the Grand Lodge of California.

What This Website Is For

This is an informational website. It exists to explain what Freemasonry is, to describe Anaheim Lodge No. 207 and its activities, to publish our calendar, and to give an interested person a straightforward way to reach us.

There are no user accounts, no logins, and nothing to buy. Nothing on this website is an offer of membership, and contacting us places no obligation on you or on us.

Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

Words with an initial capital letter have the meanings defined below. These definitions have the same meaning whether they appear in the singular or the plural.

Definitions

  • Lodge ("we", "us", "our") means Anaheim Lodge No. 207 F. & A. M., 303 W Lincoln Avenue, Anaheim, California 92805.
  • Website means this website, anaheimlodge207.org.
  • You means the individual accessing or using the Website.
  • Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Service Provider means a third party that processes data on our behalf, such as our website host or our email newsletter platform.

Information You Give Us

The Website collects Personal Data in four places, and only when you choose to type something in and press send.

1. The short contact form in the page footer

Appears at the foot of most pages. It asks for your first name, your email address, a subject, and a message.

2. The “Start the Conversation” form on the Contact page

A five-step form that asks for your first name, your email address, and your written answers to three reflective questions about your interest in Freemasonry. The three questions are optional; only your name and email are required.

3. The interest form on the Becoming a Mason page

Asks for your name and email address, and optionally your telephone number, your preferred first step, and a message.

4. The newsletter subscription

If you subscribe to our monthly Trestle Board we collect your email address, and your first name if you provide it.

Information Collected Automatically

We do not run advertising on this Website, and we do not operate any analytics or tracking service of our own. We do not set cookies to follow you between visits, and we do not build a profile of you.

Two things happen automatically that you should know about:

  • Server logs. Our website host records ordinary technical information when a page is requested, including your IP address, browser type, and the page you asked for. This is standard for every website and is used for security and to keep the site running.
  • Embedded content. The Calendar page embeds a calendar from Google, and the newsletter sign-up is embedded from Mailchimp. These embeds are served by those companies and may set their own cookies when the page loads. We do not control them and we do not receive the data they collect. Their own privacy policies apply.

Who Processes This Information

  • Netlify hosts this Website and receives form submissions, which it forwards to the Lodge Secretary by email.
  • Mailchimp stores newsletter subscriptions and sends the Trestle Board on our behalf.
  • Google serves the embedded calendar and the map links.

These are our only Service Providers. We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose your Personal Data to anyone else, and we never will.

How We Use It

  • To read your message and reply to it
  • To send you the monthly Trestle Board, if you asked for it
  • To carry on ordinary correspondence if you go on to petition for membership
  • To comply with a legal obligation, in the unlikely event that one applies

We do not use your information for any other purpose, and we do not add you to a mailing list because you used a contact form. Those are separate choices.

What We Never Ask For

This Website does not ask for your religion, your date of birth, your home address, your employer, or any financial or payment information. Questions of that kind belong to the petition process, which happens in person, not on a web form.

How Long We Keep It

Form submissions are kept for as long as needed to answer you and to hold a reasonable record of correspondence. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you at any time, and we will.

Your Choices

  • Every Trestle Board we send carries an unsubscribe link, which takes effect immediately.
  • You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the address below.
  • You can browse this entire Website without giving us anything at all.

Children

This Website is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from them. Our pages about the Order of DeMolay and Job's Daughters are written for parents and guardians, and inquiries about those programmes should come from an adult. If you believe a child has sent us Personal Data, contact us and we will remove it.

Photographs

Photographs published on this Website show members of the Lodge and its youth orders at Lodge events, and are used with their knowledge. If you appear in a photograph here and would prefer not to, tell us and we will take it down.

Links to Other Websites

We link to the Grand Lodge of California, to the national bodies of our youth orders, to Google Maps, and to our social media pages. We do not control those sites and we are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices. Please read their policies.

Security

This Website is served over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change takes effect when it is posted here, and the date at the top of this page will change with it.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or a request about your information, can go to anaheimlodge207@gmail.com or to the Lodge Secretary, Anaheim Lodge No. 207 F. & A. M., 303 W Lincoln Avenue, Anaheim, California 92805.
